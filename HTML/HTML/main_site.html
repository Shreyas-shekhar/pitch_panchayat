Why Participate?

Pitch Panchayat is more than a competition. It is a platform designed to help students transform innovative ideas into impactful startups. Participants will gain practical experience by presenting their ideas before entrepreneurs, investors, startup founders and industry mentors. Whether you already have a startup or just an exciting idea, Pitch Panchayat provides an opportunity to learn, connect, collaborate and grow alongside some of the brightest innovators in the country. Every participant leaves with valuable exposure, meaningful connections and unforgettable experiences.
