Pitch Panchayat is an entrepreneurship and innovation platform where participants get an opportunity to present their ideas, solutions and ventures to a panel of judges and industry professionals.

Students, aspiring entrepreneurs, innovators and other eligible participants can participate, subject to the eligibility requirements of the event.

Yes. Individual participation may be allowed depending on the applicable registration requirements. Participants can also participate as part of a team.

A team can consist of up to 5 participants. The required details of every team member must be submitted during registration.

Participants can present innovative ideas, startup concepts, products, services or solutions that address meaningful real-world problems.

Not necessarily. Depending on the event requirements, participants may be able to present an early-stage idea or concept without having an already registered startup.

Ideas may be evaluated on factors such as innovation, understanding of the problem, feasibility, scalability, impact, business potential and the overall quality of the pitch.

Selected participants or teams may be required to present their idea to a judging panel as part of the event.

Yes. Eligible winners may receive prizes, recognition and other opportunities associated with Pitch Panchayat. Official prize details will be communicated through the event's official channels.

Please refer to the official registration page for the current registration requirements and applicable fee information.

Once your registration has been submitted, your information will be processed according to the event procedure. Important updates, selection information and further instructions will be communicated using the contact details provided during registration.

If you need to correct or update information after submitting your registration, contact the Pitch Panchayat team as soon as possible.

Participation by minors may be subject to additional eligibility requirements, permissions or consent. Please review the applicable event terms before registering.

For questions, registration support or other enquiries, contact us at support@pitchpanchayat.in .
